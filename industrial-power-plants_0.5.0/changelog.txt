Version: 0.5.0
Date: 2026-11-02
  Features:
    - Tier 4, fast breeder with supercritical-CO2 generation (docs/FAST_BREEDER.md). Two researches: Sodium coolant
      production and Fast-breeder power.
    - Heat pipe II (2000 degC, vanilla conduction; upgrade of the vanilla heat pipe).
    - Fast-breeder reactor (300 MW, +100 % per neighbouring breeder core, warning at 2100 degC, explosion at 2500 degC): the Tier 3 control-rod script now drives both core
      types. Burns MOX or starter assemblies (new fuel category).
    - Sodium circulation unit, intermediate sodium exchanger, sodium-to-CO2 heater, supercritical CO2 compressor, CO2
      turbine-generator (150 MW), recuperator, CO2 cooler, hot-fluid storage tank, shielded fuel fabricator, sodium electrolyzer.
    - The heater chooses between a preheated-CO2 and a cold-CO2 recipe by itself (scripts/heaters.lua): without a recuperator the
      plant still runs, at 30 % instead of 50 % efficiency.
    - Blanket irradiation channel (scripts/breeders.lua): blankets are bred at the actual fission power of the core they touch.
    - Fuel cycle: MOX / starter / blanket assemblies, spent core assemblies (cooled in the Tier 3 pool), reprocessing of spent
      cores and bred blankets (breeding ratio 1.17 with blankets, 0.5 without). Plutonium-bearing material is now used.
    - Coolant chemistry in vanilla chemical plants: brine, dry sodium salt, lime kiln (lime + CO2), chlorine
      neutralization, chlorinated plastic, lime concrete. Two sodium electrolysis recipes (primary / secondary loop).
    - New components: refractory alloy, ceramic seal, precision rotor, refractory rotor, pressure housing, recuperator matrix,
      precision handling machinery, electrode assembly, thermal insulation.
    - The exhaust stack vents surplus CO2 and refuses sodium, chlorine and the working CO2 states.
    - /ipp-breeder-debug prints blanket channel state; /ipp-reactor-debug now lists both core types.
  Changes:
    - The spent-fuel pool and the reprocessing plant no longer have a fixed recipe (they take the Tier 4 recipes too).
  Bugfixes:
    - Build, clone and remove events were registered separately by the exhaust stack and reactor scripts; the engine keeps one
      handler per event, so the second registration replaced the first (stacks built after loading were never polled).
      scripts/events.lua now registers each event once for every script.

Version: 0.4.2
Date: 2026-10-30
  Features:
    - Water treatment plant (4x4, oxidizer art): three selectable recipes, water -> primary water, water -> secondary
      water and water -> cooling water. They fill the three closed loops of the pressurized-water plant, which lose
      nothing in operation.
      Unlocked by Pressurized-water nuclear power. Config: water_treatment.
    - New fluid Secondary water: the middle (steam) loop now runs on it instead of vanilla water. The nuclear steam
      generator takes it in, the surface condenser returns it. The exhaust stack refuses it.
  Changes:
    - Reworked the reactor SCRAM. A scram signal now latches the core: the rods always go all the way in at scram speed,
      even if the signal was only a pulse, and cannot be withdrawn again until a lockout has run out (new config
      reactor.scram_lockout_s, default 600 = 10 minutes, counted from the trip; 0 = no waiting). The status line shows
      the countdown. A scram signal during a lockout does not restart it, a signal still present at the end keeps the
      rods in, and cutting the wire does not end it. An unwired core still just drops its rods, without a lockout.
    - Turbine-generators (large steam, high-pressure and gas) now have only their four real fluid ports: two inputs
      (south face) and two exhaust outputs (north face). The five extra pass-through connections that the vanilla fusion
      generator's input box carries (they let the fluid leave through the input side) are removed, and each
      direction's fluid_input_graphics is cut to the 2 remaining input connections (the engine requires one entry per
      input connection). The data stage now fails with a clear message if the vanilla layout ever stops having exactly
      2 inputs and 2 outputs.
    - The cooling tower now consumes 600 vanilla water/s (cooling_tower.makeup_per_second, was 12), so it has to be
      built next to a water supply.
  Bugfixes:
    - Fuel assemblies used the pre-2.1 item field 'fuel_category'; 2.1 calls it 'fuel_categories'. Without it the
      assembly had a fuel value but no fuel category.
  Minor:
    - test_data.py: secondary-loop, cooling-tower and water-treatment checks (recipes, sprite sheet sizes, ports).
    - test_data.py: the fusion-generator mock now has the real vanilla layout (2 inputs, 5 pass-through outputs, 2
      outputs) and per-direction fluid_input_graphics (7 entries); turbines are checked for exactly 2 + 2 connections
      with the right flow direction and facing, and for one fluid_input_graphics entry per input connection.
    - test_reactors.py: SCRAM latch/lockout tests; rod speed, decay heat and meltdown expectations now follow config.lua
      (they were still on older values and 11 checks failed).

Version: 0.4.0
Date: 2026-10-29
  Features:
    - Tier 3, pressurized-water nuclear power (docs/NUCLEAR.md): reactor core, primary circulation unit, nuclear steam
      generator, high-pressure turbine train (native fusion-generator, 45 MW), surface condenser, cooling tower, dry
      cooler, spent-fuel cooling pool, dry-cask storage; optional reprocessing plant and waste vitrifier. Fuel
      assemblies, spent-fuel items, plutonium-bearing material, residue, glass frit, vitrified waste canisters.
    - Control rods are driven by circuit signals (Control-rod withdrawal 0-100 %, Reactor SCRAM). Unwired cores SCRAM by
      themselves. Rods move at limited speed, decay heat keeps heating a shut-down core, and a core that makes more heat
      than the steam side removes explodes (standard vanilla reactor explosion). New script scripts/reactors.lua and
      command /ipp-reactor-debug.
    - Two researches: Pressurized-water nuclear power, Nuclear reprocessing. The vanilla Nuclear power research no longer
      unlocks the vanilla reactor, heat exchanger or steam turbine (config replace_vanilla_nuclear).
  Changes:
    - New components: nuclear shielding, control-rod assembly, radiation-hardened controller, reinforced pipe, fuel cladding,
      chemical-resistant pipe, heating element. Recipe products may now carry a probability (helper U.stacks).
    - Exhaust stack refuses the new Tier 3 fluids.
  Minor:
    - dev-tests: test_data.py covers the Tier 3 prototypes and energy chain; new test_reactors.py simulates the control script.
